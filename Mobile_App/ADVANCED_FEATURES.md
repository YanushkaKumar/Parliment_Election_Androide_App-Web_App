## 🎯 How to Use

### View Analytics:
1. Open the app and log in
2. Select an election
3. Click "View Results"
4. Click "📈 View Detailed Analytics" button
5. Explore different chart types and filters

### Live Results:
1. From the election dialog, click "📊 Live Results"
2. Watch charts update in real-time
3. See trending party and vote count

### Dashboard:
1. Access from DashboardActivity
2. Navigate to any analytics feature
3. Quick access to all visualizations
